ENR 1 GENERAL RULES AND PROCEDURES

ENR 1.1 GENERAL RULES

The air traffic rules and procedures to GAT in the Amsterdam FIR conform to Regulation (EU) No 923/2012 (SERA), Annex 2 and 11 to the Convention on International Civil Aviation and to those portions of the Procedures for Air Navigation Services, Rules of the Air and Air Traffic Services and the Regional Supplementary Procedures applicable to the EUR-Region.
 
Additional provisions are indicated in GEN 1.7.
 
Note: the air traffic rules and procedures applicable to OAT in the Amsterdam FIR are published in the MIL AIP Netherlands.
