1 OTHER ACTIVITIES OF A DANGEROUS NATURE

1.1 Gas venting

During the venting of natural gas under high pressure at the compressor stations of the natural gas pipeline network, which is an infrequent and unpredictable event, severe turbulence and engine power fluctuations could be experienced over the gas compressor stations.

Note:  a NOTAM will be issued if, during maintenance, dangerous gas clouds may occur at other positions along the natural gas pipeline network.

1.5 UAS geographical zones

The information on the UAS geographical zones in the Amsterdam FIR, including their period of validity, is publicly available as a digital data set in JSON format as required by the Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/947 on the rules and procedures for the operation of unmanned aircraft, the EASA published Acceptable Means of Compliance (AMC) and EUROCAE Standard ED-269 Minimum Operational Performance Standard for UAS Geo-Fencing.
The effective UAS geographical zone data set is available for direct download via the URL:
https://www.nieuwsienw.nl/api/documents/downloadfile?sectionid=178144&fileid=1650029&forcedownload=true.
The UAS geographical zone data set contains information about restrictions and conditions over and in the vicinity of objects of national and public interest. More information (in Dutch) is provided on the website: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/onderwerpen/drone.
Information about permanent and temporary airspace elements that may affect the operation of UAS flights is available in a chart viewer.
The viewer is available on the website: https://map.godrone.nl.
