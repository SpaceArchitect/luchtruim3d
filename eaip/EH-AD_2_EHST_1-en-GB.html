EHST AD 2.22 FLIGHT PROCEDURES

1 VFR FLIGHT PROCEDURES AND REGULATIONS

Note:  for visual procedures and traffic circuits see chart VAC - VFR procedures.

1.1 General

  1. The minimum approach altitude is 1014 FT AMSL (1000 FT AAL).
  2. The circuit altitude is 514 FT AMSL (500 FT AAL).
  3. Approaching the aerodrome report over ECHO or WHISKEY.
  4. Built-up areas shall be avoided as much as possible.
  5. Marked areas shall be avoided.
