Tele/WebEx conferences
	
	



NM may invite Customers via AIM or Headline News to join a specific Operations Briefing targeted towards adhoc situations as they occur.

The Briefings connect operational stakeholders via conference as part of a strengthened coordination process to jointly achieved better operations.

The conferences may be in the form of a web conference via WebEx or a teleconference via InterCall.

* For the teleconference dial +32 2891 0103. The required PIN and/or additional details will be published via AIM and/or Headline News.

NM Incident eTOKAI Log in

	
For post-event occurrences users can raise claims with the NM Incident team.
eTOKAI is recognized as an easy-to-use online tool already utilized by many Air Navigation Service Providers (ANSPs) and Aircraft Operators (AO) and CFSP for occurrence investigation, supporting safety management systems and partner collaboration.

Users who wish to report post-event operational problems via eTOKAI can request access via the "contact us" page.

Select Post-event incident investigations from the dropdown menu.
In Your question, insert: eTOKAI access request.
